  • Difference between a hour and a branding? +

    • The Branding is ongoing and flexible — ideal for Startups design needs. Hour Rates are one-time, fixed-scope engagements for quicker goals like a rebrand or landing Page.

  • What types of design do you handle? +

    • Webdesign, Branding, product UI, landing pages, E-Commerce shop, brand assets, decks, social media visuals, anything digital that needs to look and feel sharp.

  • How many requests can I make? +

    • As many as you like, with a branding subscription, you can queue unlimited requests, and they’ll be handled one at a time in priority order.

  • Do you offer development too? +

    • Denqid focuses on design and Branding only, but all deliverables are dev-ready.

  • Can I pause the Branding? +

    • Yes — you can pause anytime and resume when you’re ready. Unused days roll over. But theres no Money-back guarantee.

  • How fast is the turnaround? +

    • Most hour-requests are delivered within 1–2 business days. Larger tasks may take longer, but you’ll always be kept in the loop.

  • What tools do you use? +

    • Figma for design, Framer for Development, Notion for task management, and Google or E-Mail for async communication.
